Services

From empty land to packed product.

Turnkey cold storage

Cold stores, pre-coolers, blast and spiral freezers, IQF, production lines, concentrate and juice plants, vacuum freeze-dry.

02

Design and assembly

Industrial refrigeration designed to site specifications, compliant with the ISO quality standard.

03

Testing and commissioning

System checks, start-up, compressor overhauls and PLC/SCADA upgrades.

04

Energy saving and retrofits

Turning outdated, power-hungry installations into efficient, sustainable assets.

05

Contracting and installation

Welding, fitting, sandwich panels, cladding, PU injection and power and control installation.

06

Consulting

Full tender specifications for civil, structure, refrigeration, boilers and production lines.

07

System audits and diagnostics

On-site assessments of compressors, evaporators, condensers and controls.

08

Direct equipment sales

Chillers, freezers, cold rooms, boilers, air compressors and IoT monitoring.

09

Annual maintenance contracts

Preventive and corrective maintenance and performance optimization.

10

After-sales contracts

Backed by R&D prototyping, simulation and trained technicians.

11

Smart platforms

IoT and AI monitoring and control, and cold-asset leasing for SMEs and investors.

Sustainability by design

Efficiency engineered in, not added on.

01

COP-driven design

Coefficient of performance is a core design input: less electrical power for the same cooling.

02

Natural refrigerants

Ammonia (NH₃, R717) and carbon dioxide (CO₂, R744).

03

Hybrid power continuity

Existing infrastructure combined with renewables, battery storage or generators, depending on the site.

04

Automation and UPS

Continuous SCADA access, monitoring and operational visibility.

05

Proactive fail-safes

Alerts reach the responsible teams before failures happen.

06

Service strength

Fast response, expert support and long-term maintenance contracts to maximize uptime.
