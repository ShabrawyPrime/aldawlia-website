About JET Solar

Our venture in energy, now in renewables.

Led byEng. Hazem Nabil El Shabrawy, CEO & General Manager

Since 2020 JET Solar has combined advanced manufacturing technology, rigorous quality control and continuous innovation to deliver reliable modules for residential, commercial, industrial and utility-scale applications.

Localizing manufacturing lets the company tailor modules to each client's technical and aesthetic needs, including coloured panels that combine architecture with energy, and it continues to expand capacity for Egypt and regional markets.
