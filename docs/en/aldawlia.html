Mother company

Cooling excellence, since 1986.

Aldawlia for Cooling Corporation is the engineering hub of the group. It provides consultancy, design, local contracting and maintenance for large-scale industrial refrigeration, in plants that leave no room for error.

Who we are

An engineering hub for industrial refrigeration.

With four decades of specialised experience, Aldawlia has become a primary reference for industrial refrigeration in Egypt and the Middle East. From mega refrigeration plants to precision temperature control for production lines and logistics hubs, it takes on the toughest industrial challenges.

Integrated systems are designed for maximum efficiency and minimum power consumption, backed by smart monitoring for stable operation and zero waste. That is why major factories and corporations choose Aldawlia where quality and engineering excellence are the only acceptable standards.

Inside an ammonia refrigeration plant.

  1. 1

    Screw compressor packages

    Six Mayekawa MYCOM packages with their oil separators, drawn from the manufacturer's 3D model; one on standby.

  2. 2

    Surge drums and ammonia pumps

    Three low-pressure separators at −40 °C, −28 °C and −10 °C, with pumped recirculation to the tunnels, freezers and cold stores.

  3. 3

    High-pressure receiver and priority vessel

    The liquid ammonia store fed by the condensers; the priority vessel secures the compressors' oil cooling.

  6. 6

Aldawlia · Potato cold stores

Inside a bulk potato store.

123456
  1. 1

    Fan wall and plenum

    Axial fans in the front wall push conditioned air into the floor plenum under the pile.

  2. 2

    Underground ducts

    Grated channels under the pile spread the air evenly through six metres of potatoes.

  3. 3

    The pile

    Bulk storage six metres deep in cells of 24 by 27 metres and 9.5 metres high; a loading conveyor fills each cell.

  4. 4

    Air coolers and humidification

    Ceiling-hung coolers above the fan wall hold the set temperature and humidity.

  5. 5

    Mixing and inlet louvers

    Fresh-air and recirculation dampers above the fan wall, driven by CO₂ and temperature.

  6. 6

    PLC control

    Sensor spears in the pile feed each cell's own control loop: Egypt's first fully PLC-run potato store was an Aldawlia project (2018).

Drawn from an Aldawlia bulk-store design: cells 24 × 27 m, 9.5 m high, 6 m pile, underground-duct ventilation.
