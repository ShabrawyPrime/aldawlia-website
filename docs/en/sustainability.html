Natural refrigerants

Why ammonia is the benchmark for industrial refrigeration.

CriterionAmmonia (NH₃)Freon (HFC/HCFC)
EnvironmentZero ozone depletion (ODP 0), zero global warming potential (GWP 0); naturally occurringMany HCFCs deplete ozone; HFCs have high GWP and are being phased out
EnergyHigher COP and a smaller charge; 15–20% less energy in industrial plantsLess efficient at large scale
Lifetime costInexpensive, readily available, stable in cost; long service lifeVolatile pricing under bans, taxes and phase-outs
ScaleIdeal for cold storage, food processing and petrochemicalsBetter suited to small and medium systems
SafetyToxic in high concentrations; managed with detectors, ventilation and containmentGenerally non-toxic, but many HFCs decompose into harmful gases in fire

Made in Egypt

Solar modules manufactured in Abu Rawash, and refrigeration plants localized since the 1980s: fewer imports, local jobs and up to 40% lower project cost.

Skills for the next generation

Aldawlia has trained generations of engineers and technicians. JET Solar Academy works with universities, and the Nova Freeze training centre uses Japanese test rigs with the ELARABY School of Applied Technology.

Lifecycle thinking

COP-driven design, energy-saving retrofits for ageing plants, and lifecycle carbon-footprint assessments.

Safety first

Ammonia plants are engineered with modern safety systems: gas detection, ventilation and containment, NDT-tested piping and fail-safe alarms that reach the responsible team before a fault becomes an emergency.
