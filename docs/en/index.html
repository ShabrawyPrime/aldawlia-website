Our story

Refrigeration is far more than a technical service; it is the vital backbone of industry. Our mission is to transform it through automation, making it resilient, intelligent and sustainable.

Eng. Nabil El Shabrawy, Founder

He learned the craft on site as a student, at the installation of Egypt's first industrial cooling systems with Sabroe of Denmark. He then built Aldawlia into a school for generations of engineers.

The Aldawlia way · Industrial localization

Up to40%lower total project cost

Import only the technological heart. Make everything else in Egypt.

Since the 1980s Aldawlia has imported only the core technology and sourced piping, cabling, panels and expertise locally, to international standards. The approach cut total project costs by up to 40% and made the company an academy for engineers across the Middle East.

Imported

The technological heart: compressors and core equipment from world leaders

Made in Egypt
